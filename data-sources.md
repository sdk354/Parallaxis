## Geopolitical & Conflict Data

| Source | Data type | Access | Notes |
|--------|-----------|--------|-------|
| ACLED | Armed conflict events, locations, fatalities | Free for researchers | Best conflict event data |
| GDELT | Global events, tone, geography | Fully free | Very broad coverage |
| UN Voting Data | UNGA/UNSC votes 1946–2024 | Free | Bailey et al. dataset on Harvard Dataverse |
| GeoConfirmed | Verified conflict geolocations | Free | 71,000+ verified events |
| Uppsala Conflict Data (UCDP) | Armed conflicts | Free | Academic, well-maintained |
| Crisis Group CrisisWatch | Monthly conflict updates | Free tier | Good severity ratings |
| AidData | Foreign aid, BRI loans | Free | Chinese lending data |
